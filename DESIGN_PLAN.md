# Handcrafted Haven Design Plan

## Color Schema

Use a warm, handcrafted palette that communicates trust, quality, and authenticity while maintaining strong contrast for WCAG 2.1 Level AA accessibility.

- Primary color: Deep earthy brown for headers, navigation, and key interactive elements.
  ```css
  #5C4033
  ```
- Secondary color: Soft cream for page backgrounds and large content areas.
  ```css
  #F7F1E8
  ```
- Accent color: Muted terracotta for buttons, highlights, and call-to-action elements.
  ```css
  #A65D3A
  ```
- Supporting neutral: Warm charcoal for body text and icons.
  ```css
  #3B3A36
  ```

These colors should be paired with sufficient contrast ratios, especially between text and background, to ensure readability and accessibility.

## Typography

Choose an elegant serif heading font paired with a clean, highly readable sans-serif body font.

- Headings: Playfair Display
  - Elegant and refined, suitable for artisan branding.
  - Google Fonts alternative: Playfair Display
  - Web-safe fallback: Georgia, serif
- Body text: Open Sans
  - Clear and modern, with excellent legibility on digital screens.
  - Google Fonts alternative: Open Sans
  - Web-safe fallback: Arial, sans-serif

## General Layout

Design the site with a warm, curated feel that mirrors a boutique artisan marketplace.

- Header with navigation for Home, Shop, About, Contact, and Account.
- Hero section featuring high-quality imagery of handmade goods and a prominent call-to-action.
- Grid-based product catalog with clear categories, filtering options, and featured products.
- Dedicated product detail sections with images, descriptions, pricing, and reviews.
- Footer containing contact information, social links, and copyright details.

## Graphical Elements & UI

- Rounded product cards with subtle border styling to create a polished, handcrafted feel.
- Soft hover shadows and gentle transitions to highlight interactive elements without overwhelming the interface.
- Clear, accessible call-to-action buttons with sufficient contrast and visible focus states.
- Simple iconography and form controls that support readability, keyboard navigation, and screen-reader compatibility.
